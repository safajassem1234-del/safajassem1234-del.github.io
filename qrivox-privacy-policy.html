Privacy Policy

Qrivox - QR Scanner & Generator

Last updated: March 16, 2026

📋 Introduction

Welcome to Qrivox. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application.

Qrivox is a QR code scanner and generator app designed with a privacy-first approach. The app operates primarily offline, and all user data is stored locally on your device.

📊 Information We Collect

Local Data (stored on your device only):

  • QR code scan history and generated QR codes
  • Favorite QR codes you have bookmarked
  • App settings and preferences (theme, vibration, etc.)

Important: All of this data is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on external servers.

📷 Camera Access

Qrivox requests access to your device's camera solely for the purpose of scanning QR codes in real-time. Camera data is processed entirely on your device using on-device machine learning (Google ML Kit) and is never transmitted, recorded, or stored externally.

You can use the app without granting camera permission by scanning QR codes from images in your gallery.

📢 Advertising

Qrivox uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and personalize ads, including:

  • Device advertising identifier
  • Device and network information
  • Ad interaction data

For more information about how Google handles data, please visit Google's Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's settings under "Ads" or "Privacy".

🌐 Permissions

  • Camera: Required for real-time QR code scanning (optional - gallery scan works without it)
  • Internet: Required for displaying advertisements
  • Vibration: Used for haptic feedback when a QR code is successfully scanned (can be disabled in settings)

🔒 Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously:

  • All scan history and preferences are stored locally on your device
  • No personal data is collected or transmitted to external servers
  • No user account or login is required
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are used (apart from Google AdMob for ads)
  • QR code content is processed entirely on your device

🛡 Third-Party Services

Qrivox uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google ML Kit (Barcode Scanning): For on-device QR code detection. All processing happens locally on your device. ML Kit Terms

👶 Children's Privacy

Qrivox is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If we discover that we have inadvertently collected data from a child under the specified age, we will take steps to delete it immediately.

🗑 Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can delete your data by:

  • Using the "Clear History" option within the app's History screen or Settings
  • Clearing the app data from your device's settings
  • Uninstalling the application

🔄 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically for any changes.

📧 Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, you can reach us through the developer contact page on Google Play Store.
