Velliq - Privacy Policy

Document Scanner Application

Effective Date: March 15, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Velliq ("we", "our", "the app"). Velliq is a document scanner application designed to help you scan, crop, edit, and manage documents directly on your Android device. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how the app works.

Key Point: Velliq processes all your documents locally on your device. Your scanned documents are never uploaded to any external server.

Information We Collect

We do NOT collect any personal information. Velliq does not require account creation, login, or any personal data to function.

The app uses the following device permissions:

  • Camera: Required to scan documents using your device camera. Camera data is processed entirely on-device and is never transmitted externally.
  • Internet Access: Used solely for displaying advertisements through Google AdMob. No personal document data is transmitted over the internet.

Document Data & Storage

All documents you scan, crop, edit, and save using Velliq are stored locally on your device only. Specifically:

  • Scanned images are saved in the app's private storage directory.
  • Exported PDF and image files are saved to your device's local storage.
  • Document metadata (titles, page counts, timestamps) is stored in a local database on your device.
  • No document data is uploaded, shared, or transmitted to any server or third party.
Your documents remain entirely under your control. Uninstalling the app will remove all stored data.

Third-Party Services

Velliq uses the following third-party service:

  • Google AdMob: We display advertisements to support the free availability of the app. Google AdMob may collect certain device information (such as advertising ID, device type, and general location) for the purpose of serving relevant ads. This data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not use any analytics, tracking, or data collection services beyond what is required for ad delivery.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any user data with third parties. The only external data exchange occurs through Google AdMob for advertisement purposes, as described above.

Children's Privacy

Velliq is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The app is designed as a general-purpose document scanner for all ages.

Data Security

Since all document data is stored locally on your device, data security is managed by your device's built-in security features (screen lock, encryption, etc.). We recommend keeping your device software up to date for optimal security.

Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Delete: You can delete any scanned document at any time from within the app.
  • Export: You can export your documents as PDF or image files at any time.
  • Uninstall: Removing the app will delete all associated data from your device.
  • Ad Preferences: You can manage your ad preferences through your device's Google settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
