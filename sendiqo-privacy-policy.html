Sendiqo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 22, 2026

Summary: Sendiqo is a privacy-first image utility app. All image processing is performed entirely offline on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your photos or personal data. Third-party advertising services may collect limited data as described below.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Sendiqo ("we", "our", "the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Android application. By using Sendiqo, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Sendiqo provides offline image utility tools including image compression, metadata (EXIF/GPS) removal, image-to-PDF conversion, and batch image processing. All operations are performed locally on your device.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Sendiqo is designed with privacy as a core principle. Specifically:

  • No account registration is required to use the App.
  • No personal data (name, email, phone number, address) is collected.
  • No usage analytics are collected by us directly.
  • No images or files are uploaded or transmitted to any server.
  • No metadata from your photos is collected or stored by us.

3. How Your Images Are Processed

All image processing in Sendiqo happens entirely on your device:

  • Image Compression: Images are compressed locally using your device's built-in processing capabilities. No images are sent to external servers.
  • Metadata Removal: EXIF data, GPS coordinates, and camera information are stripped locally. The removed metadata is never transmitted anywhere.
  • PDF Conversion: Images are converted to PDF files entirely on your device.
  • Batch Processing: Multiple images are processed sequentially on your device without any network activity.

Temporary files created during processing are stored in the App's private cache directory and are automatically cleaned up. You can also manually clear the cache from the App's Settings.

4. Device Permissions

Sendiqo may request access to the following device features:

  • Photo/Media Access: Required to select images for processing. Sendiqo uses Android's Photo Picker API, which provides access only to the specific images you select — not your entire photo library.
  • Storage (Android 9 and below): On older Android versions, storage permission may be requested to save processed images to your device's shared storage.
  • Internet: Used solely for displaying advertisements. No personal data or images are sent through the internet connection by the App itself.

You can revoke any permission at any time through your device's Settings.

5. Data Storage

All app preferences (theme choice, default quality, output format) are stored locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences. This data is:

  • Never transmitted to external servers.
  • Automatically removed when you uninstall the App.
  • Accessible only by the Sendiqo app on your device.

6. Third-Party Services

Sendiqo uses the following third-party service:

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the App. AdMob may collect and process certain data in accordance with Google's privacy policy, including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • General location information (based on IP)
  • App usage data related to ad interactions

This data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Advertising Policies.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting your device's Settings > Google > Ads and enabling "Opt out of Ads Personalization."

7. Children's Privacy

Sendiqo is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not contain content inappropriate for general audiences.

8. Security

Since all user data and image processing remain on your device, the security of your data depends on your device's own security measures (screen lock, encryption, etc.). We recommend keeping your device software up to date. Sendiqo's core purpose is to enhance your privacy by helping you remove sensitive metadata from your images before sharing them.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

10. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete your data: Uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data and cache.
  • Control permissions: Manage app permissions through your device's Settings.
  • Opt out of personalized ads: Adjust your ad preferences in your device's Google Settings.
  • Clear cache: Use the in-app Settings to clear temporary processed files at any time.

11. Consent

By using Sendiqo, you consent to this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
