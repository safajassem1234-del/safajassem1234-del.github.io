SnapTools — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 22, 2026

Summary: SnapTools is designed with your privacy in mind. The app works primarily offline using your device's built-in sensors and hardware. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. Third-party advertising services may collect limited data as described below.

1. Introduction

Welcome to SnapTools ("we", "our", "the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Android application. By using SnapTools, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. SnapTools is a utility toolkit app that operates primarily on your device. Specifically:

  • No account registration is required to use the App.
  • No personal data (name, email, phone number, address) is collected.
  • No usage analytics are collected by us directly.
  • No user-generated content is transmitted to our servers.

3. Device Permissions

SnapTools may request access to the following device features. All permissions are optional and used solely for specific tool functionality:

  • Camera: Used by Magnifier, Mirror, Color Picker, QR Scanner, Text Scanner (OCR), and Heart Rate Monitor tools. Camera data is processed locally on your device and is never stored or transmitted.
  • Microphone: Used by Sound Meter and Voice Recorder tools. Audio data is processed and stored locally on your device only.
  • Location: Used by Speedometer and Compass tools. Location data is used in real-time only and is never stored or transmitted.
  • Internet: Used solely for displaying advertisements. No personal data is sent through the internet connection by the App itself.

You can revoke any permission at any time through your device's Settings. The App will continue to function, but specific tools requiring those permissions may be unavailable.

4. Data Storage

All data created within the App (calculator history, notes, preferences, favorites) is stored locally on your device using Android's standard storage mechanisms (Room Database, DataStore Preferences). This data is:

  • Never transmitted to external servers.
  • Automatically removed when you uninstall the App.
  • Accessible only by the SnapTools app on your device.

5. Third-Party Services

SnapTools uses the following third-party service:

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the App. AdMob may collect and process certain data in accordance with Google's privacy policy, including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • General location information (based on IP)
  • App usage data related to ad interactions

This data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Advertising Policies.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting your device's Settings > Google > Ads and enabling "Opt out of Ads Personalization."

6. Children's Privacy

SnapTools is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not contain content inappropriate for general audiences.

7. Security

Since all user data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on your device's own security measures (screen lock, encryption, etc.). We recommend keeping your device software up to date.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete your data: Uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data.
  • Control permissions: Manage app permissions through your device's Settings.
  • Opt out of personalized ads: Adjust your ad preferences in your device's Google Settings.

10. Consent

By using SnapTools, you consent to this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
